Short version: the game needs a Google account to play. We use your email only to recognise you and deliver your PRIVATE commissions. No ads, no tracking, no sale of data. Delete anytime in-game or by email.

1. Data we collect

  • Email address (via Google sign-in / Firebase Authentication) — to sign you in.
  • Entitlement tags (e.g. c_2026_017) + private-pack keys — to know which PRIVATE girls/outfits/skills your account owns.
  • App preferences on device only (save file, settings, update check cache) — never sent to us, deleted on uninstall.

We do NOT collect: name, photo, location, contacts, phone, advertising ID, analytics, crash logs with personal data, chat messages (there is no public chat).

2. Why and legal basis (GDPR)

  • Account management + deliver PRIVATE content + free content packs (contract: you asked to play). Legal basis: performance of contract / legitimate interest.
  • Security (build check, save seal, min_version update check): legitimate interest.

Processors: Google Firebase (Authentication + Firestore, region EU — eur3 / europe-west8) acting as data processor. Packs catalog hosted on GitHub Pages (downloads only, no account data sent there).

3. Permissions

  • INTERNET — sign-in, entitlement check, content-pack download, update check.
  • VIBRATE — haptic feedback on hits.
  • Notifications (optional, Android) — local reminders only (“while you were away” mess). No data collected. Off in SETTINGS > NOTIFICATIONS.

4. What we never do

  • No ads, no ad SDK, no analytics SDK, no data sale, no profiling.
  • Email masked in-game (m•••@gmail.com), never shown to other players.
  • Free game: no real-money purchases in the app. Capsules use tickets earned by playing, odds shown in-game. Commissions are agreed and paid outside the app directly with the artist.

5. Retention

Email + tags kept in Firebase while your account exists. Local save stays on your device until uninstall (backup disallowed: allowBackup=false). Update-check cache max 14 days offline, then a check is required.

6. Delete your account and data ⭐ (Play Data safety + deletion requirement)

Option A — in the app (fastest): SETTINGS > ACCOUNT > Delete account. If Firebase asks for recent sign-in, sign in again first, then delete.
Option B — without the app (email): write to ellm0crea@gmail.com with subject DELETE KIGURUIN ACCOUNT + your User UID (SETTINGS > ACCOUNT shows it) or the Gmail you play with.
We delete email + tags + keys within 30 days and reply to confirm. Local save: uninstall the app. Request deletion by email

7. Children

You must be old enough to hold a Google account where you live, or have parental consent (as in-game TERMS). No public user content, no stranger chat, no location sharing.

8. Security

Encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Session stored encrypted on device (account.dat, device-bound key). Release builds check signing certificate; unofficial copies lose online features. No method is 100% secure — contact us immediately if you suspect abuse.

9. Your rights (EU/UK GDPR)

Access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, objection. Write to the email above — we answer within 30 days. You can complain to your local Data Protection Authority (Italy: Garante Privacy).

10. Changes

If this policy changes, we update this page and the date above. Continued play after update = acceptance.
